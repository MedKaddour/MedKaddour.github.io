Over eight years I have taught systems and cloud topics in three institutions, always with the same
bias: students should type commands on real machines rather than read about them.

---

### 🐧 Advanced Operating Systems — University of Tlemcen (2017-2020)

Bachelor-level course on Ubuntu Linux: shell and file management, networking, then C programming for
processes, scheduling, synchronization, signals, and inter-process communication.

### ☁️ Cloud Computing and Signal Processing — University of Tlemcen (2018-2019)

Master-level introductions to cloud development tooling in Java, and to signal analysis with MATLAB
applied to audio feature extraction.

### 🧱 Middleware — Nantes Université (2024)

24 hours of Master-level teaching on cloud and edge platforms: OpenStack deployment, Docker and
Kubernetes, multi-tier observability, and lifecycle adaptation. Taught with Daniel Balouek.

### 🔁 DevOps — IMT Atlantique continuing education (2025)

DevOps principles for professionals: CI/CD pipelines, Docker, infrastructure as code, and deployment
automation, with an Ansible-driven voting application as the running exercise. Taught with
Hélène Coullon.

---

### 🧑‍🎓 Supervision

I supervised three Master's internships at the University of Tlemcen on daily activity detection in
smart homes, deep clustering for non-intrusive device monitoring, and anomaly detection in
electricity consumption. Details are on the [teaching page]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}).
