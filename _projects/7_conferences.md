I served as **Co-Web Chair** for the **18th IEEE/ACM International Conference on Utility and Cloud
Computing (UCC 2025)** and the **12th IEEE/ACM International Conference on Big Data Computing,
Applications and Technologies (BDCAT 2025)**, both held in **Nantes, France**.

---

### 🛠️ What the role involved

- Building and maintaining the two conference websites with **Hugo**, deployed through **GitLab
  CI/CD** pipelines
- Structuring the content that authors and attendees actually depend on: calls for papers, important
  dates, committees, registration, venue and travel information
- Publishing the **technical program and schedule**, including workshops and keynotes, and keeping
  them in sync with the organizing committee's decisions up to the event
- Coordinating with the general, program, and publicity chairs so that deadlines and announcements
  stayed consistent across channels

---

### 🎯 Outcome

Two conference sites that stayed accurate and up to date through the whole submission-to-event cycle,
for events gathering the cloud, utility, and big data computing communities. My own paper on
[application-level observability]({{ '/projects/4_project/' | relative_url }}) was also published in
the UCC '25 proceedings.

Note that `ucc-conference.org` and `bdcat-conference.org` now redirect to the current editions of
both series; the 2025 sites are archived in the ACM proceedings linked above.
