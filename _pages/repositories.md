## Research software

<div class="signal-grid repository-grid">
  <a
    class="signal-card signal-card-link"
    href="https://gitlab.inria.fr/STACK-RESEARCH-GROUP/software/edge-to-cloud-video-processing"
    target="_blank"
    rel="external nofollow noopener"
  >
    <h3>Edge-to-cloud video processing</h3>
    <p>
      Distributed surveillance pipeline with motion detection at the edge, YOLO-based recognition in
      the cloud, OpenTelemetry instrumentation, and a feedback loop reacting to SLO violations.
      Deployed on k3s over Grid'5000. This is the artifact behind my UCC '25 paper.
    </p>
    <span class="signal-meta">Inria GitLab</span>
  </a>
  <a
    class="signal-card signal-card-link"
    href="https://gitlab.inria.fr/STACK-RESEARCH-GROUP/software/coansible-use-cases"
    target="_blank"
    rel="external nofollow noopener"
  >
    <h3>CoAnsible use-cases</h3>
    <p>
      Use-cases and tooling for CoAnsible: Grid'5000 provisioning from an Ansible inventory,
      playbook dependency extraction, and generation of Concerto assemblies from existing playbooks.
    </p>
    <span class="signal-meta">Inria GitLab</span>
  </a>
  <a
    class="signal-card signal-card-link"
    href="https://gitlab.inria.fr/VeRDi-project/galera-experiment"
    target="_blank"
    rel="external nofollow noopener"
  >
    <h3>Galera experiment</h3>
    <p>
      Deployment and reconfiguration experiments of a Galera database cluster on Grid'5000,
      comparing Concerto-based coordination with imperative automation.
    </p>
    <span class="signal-meta">Inria GitLab</span>
  </a>
  <a
    class="signal-card signal-card-link"
    href="https://gitlab.imt-atlantique.fr/ue-devops-fila2/cours-ansible-voting-app"
    target="_blank"
    rel="external nofollow noopener"
  >
    <h3>Ansible DevOps course</h3>
    <p>
      Course material and labs for the DevOps continuing-education module at IMT Atlantique, built
      around automating a multi-service voting application.
    </p>
    <span class="signal-meta">IMT Atlantique GitLab</span>
  </a>
</div>
